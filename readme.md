# 6-Week Product Development Roadmap

## Theme: Voice-First AI Applications

### Program Overview
Participants are required to build an innovative product centered around voice interaction using Google’s Gemini 2.5 Flash Native Audio Dialog capabilities. The focus should be on creating seamless, intelligent, and real-time voice-driven experiences that solve meaningful real-world problems.

This intensive, 6-week program acts as a startup incubator, taking you from a raw concept to an enterprise-ready solution. Upon successful completion of this roadmap, you will have built a complete, fully operational Voice AI product. Beyond technical implementation, this curriculum ensures your product is market-ready by equipping you with a rigorous commercial framework: you will validate user demand, perform comprehensive market sizing, formulate a go-to-market (GTM) strategy, and compile an investor-grade pitch deck.

---

## Evaluation Criteria

Projects will be evaluated not only on technical execution and creativity, but also on their potential to evolve into scalable, startup-worthy solutions. Participants are encouraged to think beyond prototypes and design products with strong user value, practical applicability, and market relevance.

Submissions are scored across three core pillars:

### 1. Technical Execution & Engineering Core
* **Native Audio Integration:** Seamless utilization of Gemini 2.5 Flash's native audio streaming capabilities (low latency, multimodal comprehension, and audio-to-audio processing without intermediate text translation).
* **System Architecture:** Robust handling of real-time state management, fallback mechanisms, conversational turn-taking, and secure external API/tool integrations.

### 2. User Value & Practical Applicability
* **Problem-Solution Fit:** Clear evidence that a voice interface drastically simplifies, accelerates, or improves the specific user workflow compared to traditional graphical user interfaces (GUIs).
* **UI/UX Design:** High conversational intuition, clear error recovery, accessibility considerations, and an engaging auditory persona.

### 3. Market Relevance & Commercial Viability
* **Scalable Business Mechanics:** A clearly articulated business model backed by rigorous top-down or bottom-up market sizing ($TAM$, $SAM$, $SOM$).
* **GTM & Defensive Strategy:** Practical customer acquisition loops, sustainable unit economics, and an understanding of competitive positioning against global foundation model providers.
* **Pitch Execution:** Clarity, narrative structure, and commercial conviction demonstrated during the final product demo and startup pitch.
